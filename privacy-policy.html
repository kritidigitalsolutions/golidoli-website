By signing up for or using Goli Doli, you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app or services.

1. Who Can Use Goli Doli

You must be 18 years of age or older to create an account or use Goli Doli. We do not knowingly allow anyone below 18 to sign up. If we find that someone under 18 has created an account, we will suspend it and delete their data.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign up or use the app, we may collect your phone number, email address, date of birth, viewing history, watchlist, preferences, payment and billing details handled by payment partners, and messages sent for support.

We may also collect device type, operating system, app version, IP address, approximate location, usage activity, cookies, and similar tracking information. If you sign in with Google, Facebook, or Apple, we may receive basic profile information such as your name and email address.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to run your account and subscription, recommend content, process payments, prevent fraud, confirm age eligibility, send important account updates, fix bugs, improve the app, and comply with legal requirements.

4. Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell your data. We may share information with service providers for hosting, analytics, customer support, payment processing, legal authorities when required, and a new owner if Goli Doli is sold or merged. Anyone receiving data from us must protect it and use it only for the intended purpose.

5. Account Deletion

You can request account deletion by emailing support@golidoli.com, using Profile > Contact Us > Delete Account in the app, or contacting the Grievance Officer. Once verified, we will permanently delete your profile, watch history, preferences, and related account data within 3 business days. Some records may be retained where required by law, such as billing records for tax compliance.

6. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember preferences, and understand how people use the app. You can manage cookies through browser or device settings, but disabling them may affect some features.

7. Your Rights

You may ask what data we have about you, correct inaccurate data, request deletion, withdraw consent, nominate someone to manage your data rights, and file a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India if your concern is not resolved. To exercise these rights, email support@golidoli.com or contact the Grievance Officer.

8. How We Keep Your Data Safe

We use reasonable security measures including encryption, access controls, and regular checks. No system is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute protection.
